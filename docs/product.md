## Why I'm building it this way

Product design is changing fast. Design systems are moving out of design tools and
into code — repos, tokens, agent instructions — and the teams moving fastest are
the ones where a designer can work there directly.

I'm closing that gap deliberately rather than waiting. My read on what lasts:

- **The ideas behind the tools, not the tools.** Versioning, writing clear
  instructions, design tokens. Those outlive whatever is current.
- **Taste doesn't automate.** AI generates many options; someone has to know which
  one is right. Structuring information and telling a story with an interface are
  still judgement calls.
- **One small project beats a hundred saved articles.** Hence this repo.
- **Where UX is going:** from designing screens to designing how an agent behaves —
  agent experience design. See [who-to-follow.md](who-to-follow.md).

Learning git, GitHub and Claude Code is a *side benefit* of building this, not the
goal. The goal is the thing itself.

## The problem

I can't follow a routine, because my days don't repeat. The baby wakes at 5, or my
husband takes the morning and I sleep longer. Either way the shape of the day is
different, and then I lose track of:

- the basics — when to shower, eat properly, have a cup of tea sitting down
- when to play with my son, and which games suit where he is developmentally
- how to keep teaching him Spanish
- when to find time to understand my own field as it changes

Existing routine and habit apps assume the day has a fixed shape. Mine doesn't.
That's the gap.

## Lean UX canvas

1. **Problem** — see above. A routine that assumes a repeating day is useless to
   someone whose day never repeats.
2. **Outcomes** — a structure that adapts to how the day actually went. Better
   planning. A way to follow both his development and mine. Time to learn without
   drowning. Clarity.
3. **Users** — me. Others in the same position are a bonus, not the target.
4. **User benefits** — clarity and planning, without the guilt that comes from a
   streak you broke at 5am.
